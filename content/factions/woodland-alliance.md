## Overview

As the Woodland Alliance, your strength isn't fueled by strongholds or armies, but by the <term>sympathy</term> of the Woodland's own denizens. While your first sympathy token can be placed in any clearing on the map (barring effects like the {%iql(url="@/factions/marquise-de-cat.md")%}Marquise de Cat{%end%}'s keep), the remainder of your sympathy tokens must be placed in clearings without sympathy that are connected to an already-sympathetic clearing, spreading organically through the Woodland while scoring you victory points each time one is placed.

The simplest way to spread sympathy is by spending cards from your <term>supporter deck</term>, a small deck of cards that lives on your faction board separate from your hand cards. The number of supporters required to spread sympathy increases the more sympathy you have on the board, but also each scores more victory points. Be careful, however: until you're more established in the Woodland, your supporter deck is limited to five cards, and new cards that would go into the supporter deck are simply discarded.

Clearings sympathetic to your cause also won't tolerate the military actions of your opponents, which will spark <term>outrage</term>. Whenever an opponent moves warriors into a clearing with a sympathy token or removes the token (such as by battling), they'll be forced to pay a card from their hand matching the clearing's suit into your supporter deck. If they can't pay, they show you their hand as proof, and you take the top card of the deck to add to your supporter deck. Placing warriors in a sympathetic clearing, however, such as by recruiting, does not spark outrage.

As your sympathy and support grow, you'll also be able to use your supporters to incite <term>revolt</term> in sympathetic clearings. Revolts are destructive, immediately removing all non-Woodland Alliance pieces and netting you victory points for any buildings or tokens removed. Revolts also establish <term>bases</term>, buildings that serve as important footing for taking more militaristic action in the Woodland. However, you only have three bases, one for each of the three clearing suits, so once you've established all three bases, you won't be able to revolt again unless a base is destroyed.

Bases grant you a number of key capabilities, the first of which is that having any bases on the map allows you to ignore the five-card limit of your supporter deck. Furthermore, bases allow you to train <term>officers</term>, warriors placed on your faction board that allow you to recruit warriors at your bases. Officers also enable you to command those warriors, including to **Organize**, swapping a warrior for a sympathy token in a clearing without one. Losing a base, however, will slash your cause's momentum, costing you half your officers (rounded up) and all the supporters in your supporter deck that match the suit of the lost base (including bird-suited supporters). Your small supply of warriors also makes it costly to establish a significant defense force, but you are aided by your knowledge of <term>Guerrilla War</term>, allowing you to take the higher dice roll in battle even when defending.
